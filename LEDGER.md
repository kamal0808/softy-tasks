# Softy Neki ledger

Softy's project ledger: Neki earned for work on Softy (kamal0808/softy). A person's share of Softy = their Neki here / all Neki here. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md. Tasks: the issues in this repo; an accepted task becomes a row here.

Units: 1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at today's market price, priced from TOPC's rate card (`ecosystem/RATE_CARD.md` in the topc repo). Work from 2025 on uses the with-AI prices; earlier work uses the hand-work prices (rate card v2.1). Rows are only ever added, never edited; corrections are new rows. Status is `provisional` until an outside judge reviews it, then `final`.
